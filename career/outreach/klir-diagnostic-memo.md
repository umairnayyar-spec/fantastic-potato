## THE MESSAGE (short version, send this first)

Hi Elaine, I've been following Klir since the growth round, and I went
through your careers page and open roles properly rather than just skimming.

Three things stood out, and they're the reason I'm writing to you rather
than applying to anything:

**1. You're hiring across at least three employment jurisdictions right now
(Toronto, remote US, Texas) on top of Irish roots.** Seven open roles at ~60
people is a big proportional intake, and multi-country hiring at that pace
is where employment-law exposure and inconsistent leveling start compounding
quietly. It rarely announces itself. It shows up 18 months later as pay
inequity and messy exits.

**2. There's no People leader on your published exec team.** I'd guess
People sits with you alongside operations and customer success. That works
at 60. It gets expensive somewhere between 80 and 120, usually right when
you're least able to spare the attention.

**3. Your recruitment experience is already a genuine asset.** One of your
Glassdoor testimonials specifically praises the hiring process
communication. That's rarer than you'd think, and it's worth protecting
deliberately as volume goes up, because it's the first thing that breaks.

Where I'd start, if this were mine: a leveling and comp framework that holds
across Ireland, the US, and Canada before the next 20 hires rather than
after; a documented hiring bar so the candidate experience survives scale;
and clarity on which employment obligations actually differ by jurisdiction
so they're designed for, not discovered.

For context on why I'd bother writing this: I ran HR across 7+
jurisdictions at Epic Games, orchestrated a two-entity legal merger in
Sweden in under 5 months including worker-union consultation, and scaled a
company from 63 to 238 people while holding attrition under 5% and cutting
hiring cycle time 33% with no agency spend. The 60-to-scale transition
across multiple countries is specifically the thing I've done.

I'm not attached to a particular shape here: full-time, or fractional while
you decide whether the role is real yet. Would a 20-minute conversation be
useful?

## Why this format (notes for Umair, not for sending)

- **It is not a CV.** The credentials appear once, near the end, as
  justification for the observations, not as the pitch itself.
- **Every claim is checkable.** If Elaine clicks through, everything holds
  up. Nothing is invented or inflated.
- **It names a real problem she already feels.** A COO absorbing People at
  60 people while hiring across 3 countries knows it's a stretch. The memo
  says the quiet part out loud without being insulting about it.
- **It offers a low-commitment door** (fractional) alongside the high one.
- **Humility is built in** ("I'd guess", "if this were mine") because
  outside-in observations can be wrong and pretending otherwise reads badly.

**If this gets a reply, that validates the approach and I'll fold the format
into the outreach skill. If it gets silence across 3 or 4 companies, the
format needs changing. Don't scale it yet.**
