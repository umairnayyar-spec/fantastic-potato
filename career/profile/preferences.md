## Scoring rubric (used by /find-jobs)

Score each opening 0–10:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Title matches a target role | 3 |
| Location/remote policy fits one of the four tracks | 2 |
| Salary at or above target (when listed) | 2 |
| Industry preferred | 1 |
| Seniority fits (senior manager–director) | 1 |
| No dealbreakers present | 1 (auto-0 overall if a dealbreaker is hit) |

Visa note: for GCC/EU roles, postings that mention visa sponsorship/relocation
support score the full location points; ones that say "must have existing
right to work" score 0 on location unless remote.

Only openings scoring **7+** go into the tracker as `sourced`; 5–6 are listed
as "borderline — your call"; below 5 are dropped silently.
