The house and grounds are yours alone for the booked time. No shared shoots.
Hours
9:00 am to 7:00 pm daily, with natural light through the day. Venue clear by 7:15 pm.
Your team
Up to 6 people per booking, including photographer and talent. No children.
Your time
Setup and pack-down sit inside your booking. After a 5-minute grace, the 30-minute add-on applies if the next slot allows.
On the day
Parking inside the property. A changing room on request, for changing only.
Styling the set
Want furniture moved? Tell our on-site team and we will help wherever we can.
House rules

No smoking and no food indoors. No confetti, glitter, smoke or open flames. No tape or nails on any surface.
